Muralista

What you get

Your lyrics, videos and pictures land where you want them in the room, not in a rectangle someone else chose. Your wall, your shapes, and the projector fills them. You do this at the venue once, then move things around from home without going back.

What it costs

One sitting at the venue, with the projector and camera standing where they will stand on the night. You mark out the surfaces you want to use and say what each one is for.

What it does not do

It never changes your files. It decides where things go in the room, not what they are.

The layout

The default fills the projector's frame: the video across the whole frame, and the lyrics over it. It costs nothing and it works. Adjusting it is where the room comes in — and it starts from the default, already placed.

Keep the default

Muralista writes the room now, and you go straight to the output.

Adjust it

The same layout, on the canvas, where you mark out the surfaces you actually have.

Output

What is beside this is Muralista's own stand-in content, never a song from the gig. Tuning is judged against the worst case, which is what the stand-in is.

Stage capture

A photograph of the stage, taken through the calibrated camera and saved with the gig. It is what lets you map at the venue and move things around from home. Authoring only — it never reaches the output window.

    Media folder

    Layer sources are looked up inside this folder, by name — the mapping stores the name, never the folder. A name the folder does not have falls back to the served directory, so a mapping made elsewhere still opens.
